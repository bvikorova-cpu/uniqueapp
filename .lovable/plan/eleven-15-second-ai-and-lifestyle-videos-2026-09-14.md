# Eleven 15-second AI and lifestyle videos

## Goal
Create eleven separate English vertical promotional videos for:
- AI Pet Translator
- Handwriting Analyzer
- Future Face
- Photo Restoration
- Stock Content Library
- Brand Builder
- Home Designer
- Beauty Studio
- Fashion Studio
- Guess My Age
- Face Insight Studio

## Implementation
1. Add concise English labels, hooks, three feature lines, closing lines, and distinct accent colors to the existing category-video catalogue.
2. Generate one original professional 9:16 image for each tool, without embedded text, logos, or interface screenshots.
3. Create slower, clearly understandable English voiceovers matched to approximately 15 seconds.
4. Register all eleven videos with 450-frame durations and human-readable export filenames.
5. Render each video at 1080×1920, 30 FPS, H.264/AAC, including the Unique logo, `uniqueapp.fun`, music, and the established intro/outro.
6. Validate duration, dimensions, codecs, audio, and representative frames before delivery.

## Technical details
- Reuse the existing Remotion category-teaser composition and visual language.
- Keep every image unique and directly relevant to its named tool.
- Export eleven individual MP4 files to the downloadable documents folder.
