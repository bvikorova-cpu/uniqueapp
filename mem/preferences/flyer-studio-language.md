---
name: Flyer Studio language and translation quality
description: Flyer Studio controls stay English; generated flyer copy uses selected language with professional native-level translation
type: preference
---
Flyer Studio's interface must remain in English regardless of the platform language setting.

The flyer's output-language selector controls only the generated flyer. Before artwork generation, all supplied marketing copy must be translated and proofread to native advertising quality while preserving names, factual details, prices, contacts, dates, URLs and legal meaning.
